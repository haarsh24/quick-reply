Welcome to **QuickReply AI**, the digital end to "What do I even say back to this?" No more staring at a blinking cursor for twenty minutes. We use high-grade AI to turn your awkward silence into charming, witty, or professional prose.

---

## 🚀 How to Use (Faster Than Typing "K")

1.  **Paste the Vibe:** Drop the message you received into the input. Whether it's a "Hey" or a 3-paragraph workplace drama, we’ve got you.
2.  **Pick Your Length:** Toggle **"Short Mode"** for some "keep it brief" energy, or stay on **Standard** for those 1-2 line masterpieces.
3.  **Choose Your Weapon (Tone):** 
    - **Casual** for the homies.
    - **Professional** for the boss (keep that paycheck safe).
    - **Roast** for the group chat chaos.
    - **Flirty** for your "special someone."
4.  **Let the AI Cook:** Hit **"Generate"**. Our Gemini 3 Flash engine will craft 5 custom-tailored options.
5.  **Copy & Ghost No More:** Click any reply to copy it instantly. Paste, send, and thrive.
